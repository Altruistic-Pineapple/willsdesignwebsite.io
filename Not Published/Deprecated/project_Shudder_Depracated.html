Shudder Home page Redesign (Concept Work)

As the front door for the app, I designed this experience to immerse users in the wide selection of horror, thriller, and supernatural movies Shudder offers. Various swimlane styles, highlighted content cards, and interactive collection components allow Shudder to display its content in many ways. I built Breakpoints for Smart TV, Mobile App, and Desktop.
